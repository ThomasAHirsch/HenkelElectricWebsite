About Henkel Electric

Family-Run. Lake County strong.

Henkel Electric is proud to be a family-run business. We're based in Lake County, Illinois, and we serve all areas of Lake, Cook, McHenry and DuPage Counties.

We have extensive experience in new home construction and in remodeling older homes, plus a broad knowledge of commercial and industrial properties: restaurants, hotels, offices, commercial storage and nursing homes.
