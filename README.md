# HenkelElectricWebsite
This is a web app I am creating for a local electrical contractor. This business had an outdated website from 2011 that didn't prominently feature contact information. Their front-end was out of date and featured an Adobe Flash section that was no longer in service. I found the website after googling the business and knew that it needed a rework. My goal was to refresh the application to improves the user's experience when researching the business and connecting with the business owner. The new web app gives the business a refreshed modern presence that increases user traffic.
